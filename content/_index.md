# Welcome to Transgender HRT Information

This site provides basic information about male-to-female (MTF) hormone replacement therapy (HRT). The information here is for educational purposes and should not replace consultation with qualified healthcare providers.

<div class="notice">
<strong>Important Notice:</strong> Hormone therapy is a medical treatment that requires proper medical supervision. Always work with qualified healthcare providers who are experienced in transgender care.
</div>

## What is MTF HRT?

Male-to-female hormone replacement therapy involves taking medications to develop physical characteristics that align with a feminine gender identity. The treatment typically involves:

- **Estrogen** - to develop feminine characteristics
- **Anti-androgens** - to suppress testosterone and masculine characteristics

## Important Considerations

Before starting HRT, it's important to:

1. Consult with healthcare providers experienced in transgender care
2. Understand the effects, risks, and benefits
3. Consider fertility preservation if desired
4. Plan for regular monitoring and follow-up care
5. Have realistic expectations about the timeline and extent of changes

## Disclaimer

The information on this site is for educational purposes only and does not constitute medical advice. Every person's experience with HRT is unique, and outcomes can vary. Always consult with qualified healthcare professionals for personalized medical guidance.
