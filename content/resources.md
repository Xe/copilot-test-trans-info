# Resources and References

This page provides additional resources for learning about transgender hormone therapy and accessing support.

## Medical Organizations and Guidelines

### World Professional Association for Transgender Health (WPATH)
- **Website:** [wpath.org](https://www.wpath.org)
- Publishes the Standards of Care for transgender health
- International organization of healthcare professionals
- Evidence-based guidelines for treatment

### The Endocrine Society
- Clinical practice guidelines for transgender hormone therapy
- Scientific research and clinical recommendations
- Resources for healthcare providers

### Fenway Health
- **Website:** [fenwayhealth.org](https://fenwayhealth.org)
- Provider of comprehensive LGBTQ+ healthcare
- Educational resources and research
- Located in Boston but provides national resources

## Safety and Harm Reduction

### For Those Without Access to Medical Care

If you cannot access traditional medical care:

1. **Seek informed consent clinics** - More accessible than traditional gatekeeping
2. **Telemedicine options** - May be available in your area
3. **Planned Parenthood** - Many locations offer HRT services
4. **LGBTQ health centers** - Often have sliding scale fees

**If considering DIY HRT:**
- Understand the risks involved
- Use reliable sources and testing
- Monitor your health as much as possible
- Seek medical care when available
- r/TransDIY has harm reduction information
- **This site does not endorse DIY HRT but recognizes that some individuals lack access to traditional care**
